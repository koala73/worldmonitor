This guide is published by World Monitor. We compare public product documentation and our own documented capabilities. Recommendations are editorial judgments, not a hands-on benchmark. World Monitor's capabilities were rechecked on October 18, 2026, and competitor sources on October 2, 2026. Confirm current access and contracts before buying.

## TL;DR

> The right Recorded Future alternative depends on which module you are replacing. If you mainly use geopolitical monitoring, choose World Monitor: a free live map, alert rules by country, event type and severity delivered to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram, email, browser push or a signed webhook, and a REST API, SDKs and MCP at published prices. Choose Dataminr for enterprise event detection around people and assets. Choose Factal for alerts verified by a 24/7 editorial team. Stay with Recorded Future if you depend on its cyber threat intelligence.

## Why teams leave Recorded Future

Teams usually leave over cost or scope. Some license the Geopolitical Intelligence module but do not use the cyber side. Others want to try a tool without a module negotiation, or route alerts and data into their own systems. A replacement for a geopolitical overview is not a replacement for cyber investigation, enrichment, or a security integration.

## 1. World Monitor

**Best when you are replacing geopolitical monitoring on cost and self-serve access.**

World Monitor publishes its prices on the [plans page](https://www.worldmonitor.app/pro#pricing), and you sign up without a sales call. The [free map](https://www.worldmonitor.app/dashboard) shows conflict, protests, shipping, aviation, infrastructure outages, hazards, cyber and markets, with live ship transit counts at 13 [maritime chokepoints](https://www.worldmonitor.app/chokepoints). On the Pro plan, alert rules filter by event type, severity (all, high or critical) and country and deliver to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram, email, browser push, or a webhook signed with HMAC-SHA256, in real time or as a digest. Real-time alerts link to the original report, and email digests link every item ([notifications reference](https://www.worldmonitor.app/docs/api-notifications)). Developers get a REST API on a separate API plan, Python, Go and Ruby SDKs, an npm CLI, and an MCP server with Pro. Each outlet has an editorial tier and a propaganda-risk band: the News panel shows them as badges, the free `get_sources` MCP tool returns them for any outlet, and the [data sources page](https://www.worldmonitor.app/docs/data-sources) explains the scheme.

The tradeoff: no threat-intelligence module, no analyst service and no published SLA. Alerts are scoped by country, not by a radius around a facility, and World Monitor does not verify events on the ground.

## 2. Dataminr

**Best for corporate event detection around people and assets.**

Evaluate its [corporate security product](https://www.dataminr.com/products/corporate-security/) when real-time detection around people and facilities drives the purchase. It is another enterprise contract, with a published [service-level agreement](https://www.dataminr.com/legal/sla/), so it solves scope rather than cost.

## 3. Factal

**Best for verified incident alerts.**

Factal detects events with AI and verifies them with a [24/7 team of journalists](https://www.factal.com/about/). A [flat team fee](https://www.factal.com/roi/) covers unlimited users, and its [API](https://www.factal.com/solutions/security-ai-safety-data/) offers live and historical incident data back to 2018. Factal does not publish its price.

## 4. Crisis24

**Best for people and travel risk operations.**

Evaluate [Horizon](https://www.crisis24.com/platforms/crisis24-horizon) when the downstream action includes traveler communication or assistance. Ask for a demonstration of the full workflow and service responsibilities.

## Stay with Recorded Future if

Stay with Recorded Future if your security program relies on its [Threat Intelligence](https://www.recordedfuture.com/products/threat-intelligence) module, investigations or integrations alongside [Geopolitical Intelligence](https://www.recordedfuture.com/products/geopolitical-intelligence). It also documents [MCP tools](https://support.recordedfuture.com/hc/en-us/articles/55777121249043-Recorded-Future-MCP-Available-Tools) that follow your licensed modules. None of the alternatives here replaces cyber threat investigation.

## Pick World Monitor when

- **You use the geopolitical side, not the cyber side.** One map covers conflict, shipping, aviation, outages, hazards, cyber events and markets.
- **You want alerts without a module negotiation.** Rules by country, event type and severity deliver to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram, email or browser push.
- **Your system or AI agent consumes the data.** A signed webhook, REST API, SDKs and MCP server cover it.
- **You need a price before a call.** World Monitor publishes its prices.

## Our pick

If you are leaving Recorded Future because you only use geopolitical monitoring and want a published price, pick World Monitor. If you need detection around facilities under an SLA, pick Dataminr. If you need every alert verified by an editor, pick Factal. If your team investigates cyber threats, stay with Recorded Future.

## What to do before switching

List the intelligence modules, API calls, agent tools and security integrations your team uses today. Map each requirement to a demonstrated replacement. If one essential investigation still requires Recorded Future, keep that module rather than claiming a full migration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a free Recorded Future alternative?**

World Monitor's map is free with no account. Alert rules, digests and MCP need the Pro plan, and the REST API is a separate API plan. It is not a replacement for cyber threat intelligence.

**Does any alternative offer MCP?**

World Monitor's MCP server comes with Pro. We have not verified MCP servers for Dataminr or Factal.

**What should a migration prove?**

The replacement must answer your geopolitical questions with acceptable coverage and deliver alerts where your team acts. Document every requirement that still needs Recorded Future.
