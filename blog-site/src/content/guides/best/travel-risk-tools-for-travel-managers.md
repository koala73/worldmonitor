> For travel managers, the best travel risk tool is the one that covers duty of care: knowing who is affected, reaching them, and getting them help. Choose Crisis24 for that full job, because it documents traveler visibility, mass notification and assistance. Choose Dataminr when your gap is enterprise event awareness around people and assets. ## What matters for travel managers

The travel manager's question is not only what happened. It is who is affected, how to contact them, and where help comes from. Product selection must cover that service chain, including responsibilities outside the software interface. A research tool can sit beside that chain but cannot replace it.

## Different jobs need different tools

### People and trip visibility

Check how the team identifies affected travelers and handles access to personal information. Demonstrate the workflow with a representative trip.

### Communication and help

Confirm who sends a notice, handles an acknowledgment and arranges assistance. Read the actual service scope for each region.

### Destination research

Use intelligence as an input to your organization's process. Check the original advisories and update times before making a recommendation.

## 1. Crisis24

**Best for travel-risk management and assistance.**

Crisis24 Horizon documents travel risk, people and site visibility, mass notification and assistance. That makes it the strongest match in this selection for the travel manager's full duty-of-care task. Coverage and service levels depend on the contract. We have not tested the response service.

[Official Crisis24 documentation](https://www.crisis24.com/platforms/crisis24-horizon).

## 2. Dataminr

**Best for enterprise awareness of disruptive events.**

Choose Dataminr if your primary gap is discovering events affecting people or assets, under an enterprise contract with an availability SLA. Its corporate-security scope addresses that problem. Confirm travel-support responsibilities separately; event awareness does not include an assistance service.

[Official Dataminr documentation](https://www.dataminr.com/products/corporate-security/).

## 3. World Monitor

**Best for destination research and country alerts.**

World Monitor puts conflict, protests, aviation, shipping, infrastructure outages, hazards, cyber and markets on one map, free with no account at [worldmonitor.app/dashboard](https://www.worldmonitor.app/dashboard). That covers the research behind a destination brief. On the paid Pro plan, alert rules filter by country, event type and severity (all, high or critical) and deliver to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram, email, browser push or an HMAC-SHA256 signed webhook. Real-time alerts link to the original report, and email digests link every item ([notifications reference](https://www.worldmonitor.app/docs/api-notifications)). Prices are published at [worldmonitor.app/pro](https://www.worldmonitor.app/pro#pricing).

It does not know where your travelers are, cannot message them, and provides no travel or evacuation assistance, analyst desk or SLA. Alerts are scoped by country, not by a radius around a hotel or office. Use it beside official advisories and your travel-risk provider.

## Our pick

If you own the complete travel-risk workflow, pick Crisis24. If event awareness around people and assets is the missing part, pick Dataminr without discarding existing assistance arrangements. If you write destination briefs and want country alerts at a published price, pick World Monitor, and keep a separate provider for traveler tracking and assistance.

## What to do today

1. Choose a representative trip and list the traveler-support responsibilities your team must fulfill. Include the out-of-hours handoff.
2. Ask the provider to demonstrate a disruption, the message to the traveler and the assistance route. Record who acts at each stage.
3. Prepare a separate destination brief using original advisories and public context. Make sure the research tool and operational service have clearly assigned roles.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does a country risk score authorize a trip?**

No. A score is an input with its own method and limits. Your organization's travel process must consider current conditions, the traveler's circumstances and the available support.

**Can World Monitor notify travelers or arrange evacuation?**

No. It alerts your team by country, event type and severity, but it does not track or message travelers and offers no travel or evacuation assistance. A duty-of-care provider such as Crisis24 covers that.
