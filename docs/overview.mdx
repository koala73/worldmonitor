World Monitor runs specialized variants from a single codebase, each optimized for different monitoring needs.

## Platform Variants

| Variant | URL | Focus |
|---------|-----|-------|
| **🌍 World Monitor** | [worldmonitor.app](https://www.worldmonitor.app) | Geopolitical intelligence, military tracking, conflict monitoring, infrastructure security |
| **💻 Tech Monitor** | [tech.worldmonitor.app](https://tech.worldmonitor.app/dashboard) | Technology sector intelligence, AI/startup ecosystems, cloud infrastructure, tech events |
| **😊 Happy Monitor** | [happy.worldmonitor.app](https://happy.worldmonitor.app/dashboard) | Curated positive news, global progress tracking, science breakthroughs, conservation wins |
| **💰 Finance Monitor** | [finance.worldmonitor.app](https://finance.worldmonitor.app/dashboard) | Global markets, stock exchanges, central banks, commodities, forex, crypto, economic indicators |
| **⛏️ Commodity Monitor** | [commodity.worldmonitor.app](https://commodity.worldmonitor.app/dashboard) | Commodity markets, mining sites, processing plants, supply chains, trade flows |
| **⚡ Energy Monitor** | [energy.worldmonitor.app](https://energy.worldmonitor.app/dashboard) | Oil & gas pipelines, storage facilities, tanker positions, chokepoints, energy infrastructure and shocks |

A compact **variant switcher** in the header allows seamless navigation between variants while preserving your map position and panel configuration.

## World Monitor (Geopolitical)

The primary variant focuses on geopolitical intelligence, military tracking, and infrastructure security monitoring.

### Key Capabilities

- **Conflict Monitoring** - Active war zones, hotspots, and crisis areas with real-time escalation tracking
- **Military Intelligence** - Registered military bases, flight tracking, naval vessel monitoring, surge detection
- **Infrastructure Security** - Undersea cables, pipelines, datacenters, internet outages
- **Economic Intelligence** - FRED indicators, oil analytics, government spending, sanctions tracking
- **Natural Disasters** - Earthquakes, severe weather, NASA EONET events (wildfires, volcanoes, floods)
- **AI-Powered Analysis** - Focal point detection (AI-detected news convergence zones, distinct from static intelligence hotspots), country instability scoring, infrastructure cascade analysis

### Intelligence Panels

| Panel | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **AI Insights** | LLM-synthesized world brief with focal point detection |
| **AI Strategic Posture** | Theater-level military force aggregation with strike capability assessment |
| **Country Instability Index** | Real-time CII v14 stability scores and signed 24-hour movement deltas for 31 Tier-1 countries |
| **Strategic Risk Overview** | Composite risk score combining all intelligence modules |
| **Infrastructure Cascade** | Dependency analysis for cables, pipelines, and chokepoints |
| **Live Intelligence** | GDELT-powered topic feeds (Military, Cyber, Nuclear, Sanctions) |
| **Metals & Materials** | Metals and materials quotes from Yahoo Finance, with tabs for physical premiums and EUR FX when that data is available. The gold-in-other-currencies tab appears only in the commodity variant. Energy prices appear in the Energy Complex panel instead |
| **Toronto Safety** | Toronto Police Service open data: reported major-crime occurrences (approximate, offset locations) and annual calls attended by division and neighbourhood. Not live dispatch. Off by default |
| **Global Giving** | Published giving benchmarks from platforms and institutions (GoFundMe, GlobalGiving, JustGiving, Candid, OECD), with source periods and verification status. Annualized figures are estimates, not live activity. Off by default here; on in the happy variant |

### News Coverage

Curated, attributed news feeds cover geopolitics, defense, energy, think tanks, and regional news across the Middle East, Africa, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific.

| Panel | Description |

## Energy Monitor

The energy variant ([energy.worldmonitor.app](https://energy.worldmonitor.app/dashboard)) focuses on oil and gas infrastructure, supply disruptions, and energy shocks.

### Energy Layers

| Layer | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Live Tanker Positions** | Live AIS positions of tankers within about 2° of Hormuz, Suez, Bab el-Mandeb, Malacca, Panama, and the Bosphorus. Amber markers are anchored, cyan are underway, grey have no reported speed. Polled every 60 seconds while the layer is on |
| **Pipelines** | Oil and gas pipelines from the evidence-backed registry, coloured by status (flowing, reduced, offline, disputed). On the flat map, selecting one opens the Pipeline Status drawer |
| **Storage Facilities** | Underground gas storage, strategic reserves, LNG terminals, and crude hubs from the Storage Atlas registry |
| **Fuel Shortages** | Countries with confirmed or watch-level fuel shortages from the Fuel Shortage Registry |
| **Commodity Ports** | Mineral export and import ports and terminals |

### Energy Panels

The asset registries behind these panels are curated from operator disclosures, regulator filings, and public datasets. Each status badge is derived from an evidence bundle, and selecting a row, or a marker on the flat map, opens a drawer with that evidence. Methodology: [pipelines](/methodology/pipelines), [storage](/methodology/storage), [fuel shortages](/methodology/shortages), [disruptions](/methodology/disruptions).

| Panel | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Global Energy Risk Overview** | One row of tiles: Hormuz transit status, EU gas storage fill, Brent price with its daily change, the count of active disruptions, data freshness, and a day-of-crisis counter. Each tile loads on its own and shows "—" when its source fails |
| **Oil & Gas Pipeline Status** | Asset, route, capacity, and a status badge (flowing, reduced, offline, or disputed). The drawer shows the evidence, sanctions references, and the asset's disruption timeline |
| **Strategic Storage Atlas** | Underground gas storage, strategic petroleum reserve caverns, LNG terminals, and tank farms, with country, type, capacity, and status |
| **Global Fuel Shortage Registry** | Country and product, start date, evidence, and a severity badge: confirmed (red) or watch (amber). Confirmed shortages sort first |
| **Energy Disruptions Log** | Pipeline and storage outages, filterable by cause (sabotage, sanction, mechanical, maintenance, war, weather, commercial) with an ongoing-only toggle. Selecting an event opens the affected asset's drawer |
| **GCC Energy Investments** | The GCC investment register described under [Finance Panels](#finance-panels) |

Global Energy Risk Overview is on by default only in the energy variant. The other four energy panels are also on by default in the geopolitical variant.
