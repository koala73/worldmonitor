# Cross-border movement sources

Issue #9023 extends the ODP situation view with independently published route reports. Counts keep their source, date and measure. Stocks, arrivals, returnees, deaths and enforcement encounters must not be added together. Countries and situations can overlap.

The UK series returns rolling 7- and 30-day totals only when every day in that window is present. A missing observation is unknown; an explicitly reported zero remains zero. Thirty-day change compares two disjoint 30-day windows. Revisions in the daily table replace the same date in the ODS history. A weekly surge requires more than 10,000 arrivals, or at least 1,000 arrivals and twice the median of prior complete weekly windows over 90 days, with at least three complete baseline windows. Cross-source signals use the source report clock. CII v13 adds five points once per explicitly named country for fresh arrival surges, including the existing ODP monthly arrival acceleration. Stocks and CBP encounters cannot trigger it.

Frontex nationality and border-type rows are summed once per route and month. Multi-country routes have no single country attribution. Frontex detections use the encounter measure and cannot trigger an arrival surge. Panama uses the first regional table's monthly totals, not its year-to-date total. A partial current month is omitted. Missing published observations reject the report; future month placeholders do not become zeros. A monthly arrival surge requires at least 1,000 arrivals and twice the median of three preceding complete months. Existing cross-source signals retain their weekly UK-only criterion.

`GetCrossBorderMovements` reads only Redis and supports a two-letter `country_code` and exact `route` ID. Country input is case-insensitive; assigned ISO codes are not checked, so an unmatched value returns no rows. The projection retains at most 1,000 valid movements per source, in report order, and marks a truncated source partial. One large source cannot consume another source's allowance. The generated response schema caps movements at 5,000 and source statuses at 5. The unfiltered shared request is public; filtered requests use the existing authenticated API path. MCP `get_displacement_data` retains its annual default. Set `view=movements` for operational reports, `countries` and `route` for filters, and `limit=0` for all projected rows. The default movement limit is 30, so it is not a global coverage inventory. Licence, credit and transformation notice survive projections. Stock changes retain their observation interval and remain distinct from arrival totals.

Each new source has its own key, original fetch clock, report clock, seed metadata, activation marker and last-good policy. Health stays activation-gated before the first successful publication. A failing source cannot suppress another source in the movement API. CII instead rejects builds on Redis read errors and retains last-good scores; genuine missing source keys remain unavailable inputs. The API keeps stale reports labeled as stale; the map excludes them and draws deaths separately. ODP country points without a complete monthly observation are omitted rather than mislabeled with a year-to-date total.

## Acceptance limits

Read-only local downloads and parser checks succeeded for Frontex and Panama on 2026-10-20. Frontex returned nine route totals for 2026-08-31, including explicit zeros for Black Sea and Other routes. Panama returned 71 arrivals for September, ending 2026-09-30, against a year-to-date total of 539. The earlier UK check ended 2026-10-18; CBP ended 2026-08-31. All 16 curated ODP situations were readable, but the newest Americas report was 2025-12-31, DRC 2026-04-30 and Central Asia 2026-06-30. These old reports remain visibly stale. Consequently, 15 fresh routes/situations on four continents are **not accepted**. IOM Missing Migrants/HDX remains inactive. IDMC internal-displacement events have a separate HDX worker added in #9444 and do not supply this cross-border route view. This change must not close #9023.

No production seeds, deployment, activation or live acceptance run was performed. After authorized deployment, the operator must inspect `seed-meta:displacement:border-uk`, `seed-meta:displacement:border-cbp`, `seed-meta:displacement:border-frontex`, `seed-meta:displacement:border-panama`, their canonical keys and `/api/health`. Require source-specific fetch/content clocks and valid rows over two daily runs. Railway workers must use Node.js 24 as required by this repository. Check that a failure preserves the original clock and another source remains readable. Stop the affected worker on parser or licence failures. Do not convert unavailable or missing periods into zero.
