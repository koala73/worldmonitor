The UK series returns rolling 7- and 30-day totals only when every day in that window is present. A missing observation is unknown; an explicitly reported zero remains zero. Thirty-day change compares two disjoint 30-day windows. Revisions in the daily table replace the same date in the ODS history. A weekly surge requires more than 10,000 arrivals, or at least 1,000 arrivals and twice the median of prior complete weekly windows over 90 days, with at least three complete baseline windows. Cross-source signals use the source report clock. CII v14 adds five points once per explicitly named country for fresh arrival surges, including the existing ODP monthly arrival acceleration. Stocks and CBP encounters cannot trigger it.

Frontex nationality and border-type rows are summed once per route and month. Multi-country routes have no single country attribution. Frontex detections use the encounter measure and cannot trigger an arrival surge. Panama uses the first regional table's monthly totals, not its year-to-date total. A partial current month is omitted. Missing published observations reject the report; future month placeholders do not become zeros. A monthly arrival surge requires at least 1,000 arrivals and twice the median of three preceding complete months. Existing cross-source signals retain their weekly UK-only criterion.

`GetCrossBorderMovements` reads only Redis and supports a two-letter `country_code` and exact `route` ID. Country input is case-insensitive; assigned ISO codes are not checked, so an unmatched value returns no rows. The projection retains at most 1,000 valid movements per source, in report order, and marks a truncated source partial. One large source cannot consume another source's allowance. The generated response schema caps movements at 5,000 and source statuses at 5. The unfiltered shared request is public; filtered requests use the existing authenticated API path. MCP `get_displacement_data` retains its annual default. Set `view=movements` for operational reports, `countries` and `route` for filters, and `limit=0` for all projected rows. The default movement limit is 30, so it is not a global coverage inventory. Licence, credit and transformation notice survive projections. Stock changes retain their observation interval and remain distinct from arrival totals.

Each new source has its own key, original fetch clock, report clock, seed metadata, activation marker and last-good policy. Health stays activation-gated before the first successful publication. A failing source cannot suppress another source in the movement API. CII instead rejects builds on Redis read errors and retains last-good scores; genuine missing source keys remain unavailable inputs. The API keeps stale reports labeled as stale; the map excludes them and draws deaths separately. ODP country points without a complete monthly observation are omitted rather than mislabeled with a year-to-date total.

### UNHCR Routes Monitor export research

The [UNHCR Routes Monitor working-group page](https://data.unhcr.org/en/working-group/496) embeds a public Power BI report. Its About page describes monthly consolidation of government and UNHCR reports, with source links on country pages and differing national reporting cycles. The report inspected on 2026-10-20 says “Updated: Oct 2026”, while its route overview includes August observations. The publication date cannot replace the observation period.

No supported CSV/XLSX download or documented dataset endpoint was found on that landing page or the report's About and Routes in Focus pages. [Microsoft's Publish to web limitations](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/collaborate-share/service-publish-to-web#considerations-and-limitations) explicitly exclude exporting visual data. A readable chart is not an established worker export. The report directs questions to `ams-dips@unhcr.org`; no message has been sent.

The next external dependency is a publisher-provided CSV/XLSX or documented endpoint, with route/country, indicator, observation period, count, source, revisions, cadence and reuse terms. Confirm monthly versus quarterly reporting per route before choosing freshness budgets. Keep stocks, arrivals, departures, encounters and deaths separate. Until that export is supplied and tested, do not infer missing periods as zero or enable a new feed by scraping chart labels.
