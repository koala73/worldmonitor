### 1. Find out why brand searches click through less often

- Evidence: Search Console 28d brand CTR fell from 59.7% (2026-10-06 to 2026-11-02) to 56.5% (2026-10-13 to 2026-11-09) while brand impressions rose from 62,770 to 68,241 and brand position moved from 1.24 to 1.31. The query "world monitor" fell from 68.4% (18,817 of 27,492) to 63.3% (19,479 of 30,789). The site CTR fell from 16.7% to 14.3% as impressions rose 23.2%: at the earlier week's brand, non-brand and anonymized CTRs, this week's mix gives 15.3%, so the mix explains 1.3 of the 2.3 points and lower CTR inside each segment, led by brand, explains the rest.
- Experiment: By hand, compare the result Google shows for "world monitor" with the homepage title and description, and look for a same-name site, sitelink change or result feature taking the click. Change one thing at a time and note the date. Do not scrape result pages.
- Success criteria: From 2026-11-17 the reviewed-query rows for q26 "world monitor" and q27 "worldmonitor" are reported every week, and q26 returns to a 28d CTR of at least 68% at position 1.3 or better.
- Query IDs: q21

### 2. Get the declined documentation pages indexed or retired

Bing reports 1,613 URLs in its index, but it counts every URL it holds, not the declared set, so the two numbers are not comparable.
- Experiment: List the 51 crawled-and-declined documentation URLs from the URL Inspection results, then merge, improve or noindex each one, and keep the sitemap to the pages that remain.
- Success criteria: The Documentation page-family row shows indexed pages rising from 345 toward the declared count, and the site indexedPages rises from 896, with no indexed page lost in other families.

## Reproduction contract

1. Use the same query text from `query-set.json`; do not paraphrase between platforms.
2. Record UTC date/time, country-level geography, locale, device, and whether the
   surface was signed-out or signed-in. Do not record account identifiers or prompts
   unrelated to this reviewed query set.
3. Count a direct citation only when the answer links to a `worldmonitor.app` URL.
   A mention supported only by a directory, review, or social post is not a direct citation.
4. Export Search Console/Bing data through supported product exports or APIs. Do not
   scrape search-result pages or commit property IDs, tokens, or credentials.
5. Reconcile referral aggregates with the analytics provider and keep prompt text out
   of analytics. Use bounded source/page-family fields and existing UTM attribution.

## Guardrails

- Do not automate unauthorized search-result scraping.
- Do not treat an AI mention without a worldmonitor.app source link as a direct citation.
- Do not claim causal uplift from a single observation.
- Do not store personal prompts, account identifiers, or sensitive query context.
- Do not convert unavailable data to zero or estimate it from unrelated public results.
- Do not expand the content corpus solely because an SEO vendor assigns a score.
- Use UTM fields for internal acquisition attribution; ref= remains affiliate attribution.
