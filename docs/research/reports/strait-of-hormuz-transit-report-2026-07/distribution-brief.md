# Distribution brief — Strait of Hormuz Transit Report, July 2026

**Status: no outreach has been sent.** This brief only identifies audiences for
authentic, manual outreach. Any message requires separate explicit approval
from the repository owner before sending (per #5668 guardrails), and nothing
here may be automated. No dofollow placements, paid links, or link exchanges.

Report: https://www.worldmonitor.app/research/strait-of-hormuz-transit-report-2026-07/
Angle: original, recomputable numbers on the five-month transit collapse —
1,400 transits since March 1 versus 14,547 in the same 2025 window, from IMF
PortWatch data, with the AIS-undercount caveat stated up front and the full
daily series downloadable as CSV/JSON.

## Why anyone would care

The report gives journalists and researchers something they cannot easily get
elsewhere in one place: a dated, versioned, citable series with per-figure
provenance and downloadable data — not a screenshot of a dashboard. The honest
dark-shipping limitation is a feature for this audience, not a weakness.

Individual reporters move beats; target the desk and let a human pick the
right byline at send time.

## Suggested manual sequence (after approval, one at a time)

1. Courtesy note to IMF PortWatch crediting the data and pointing to the
   attribution block.
2. One maritime trade outlet with the two strongest figures and the CSV link.
3. One community post (Hacker News or r/supplychain) written as a summary with
   the limitation stated, not as marketing copy.
4. Individual researcher/OSINT mentions only where there is a genuine reason
   to think the download is useful to their current work.

## Explicit non-goals

- No mass emailing, no automated posting, no follower campaigns.
- No pitching the same outlet twice for this edition.
- No framing beyond what the report states — in particular, nothing that
  presents AIS-observed counts as total physical flow.
