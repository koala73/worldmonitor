_Methodology maintained by [Elie Habib](https://www.worldmonitor.app/blog/authors/elie-habib/), founder of World Monitor. Published revisions are recorded in the [corrections log](/corrections)._

The [trust page](https://www.worldmonitor.app/trust/) has a monthly table of
how stories first labelled **single publisher** or **corroborated** were
labelled 24 hours and 7 days later. This page defines every number in it.

<Note>
**Coverage, not accuracy.** A label says how widely a story is carried, never
whether it is true. A story that moves from single publisher to corroborated
was later carried by two or more publisher families; that does not mean it was
confirmed. A story that moves the other way was later carried by only one; that
does not mean it was wrong.
</Note>

## Definitions

These are version 1 of the definitions (`definitionsVersion: 1` in the API).

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Label** | The coverage label the full English digest gives a story: corroborated, single publisher, tier 4 only, or not enough evidence. See [News Credibility Score](/methodology/news-credibility) and the [trust page](https://www.worldmonitor.app/trust/). |
| **First label** | The label a story carries in the first digest cycle that creates its story record. If a story's first label is logged more than once, the earliest counts and later ones count as changes. Only stories whose first label is single publisher or corroborated are counted. A story first labelled tier 4 only or not enough evidence is not in the table. |
| **Cohort day** | The UTC day of a story's first label. A story belongs to exactly one cohort day. |
| **Checkpoint** | First label time plus 24 hours, and plus 7 days. |
| **Label at a checkpoint** | The last label the digest recorded for the story at or before the checkpoint. A story that has left the digest keeps the last label it had. |
| **Same label** | The label at the checkpoint equals the first label. A story that changed and changed back by the checkpoint counts as same. |
| **Label changed** | The label at the checkpoint is the other tracked label. From single publisher: carried by two or more publisher families. From corroborated: carried by only one. |
| **Other label** | The label at the checkpoint is tier 4 only or not enough evidence. |
| **n** | The number of stories with that first label in the month's counted cohort days (`stories` in the API). For every row, same + changed + other = n. |
| **Low n** | n below 30. The row shows its counts ("9 of 12") without a percentage, and is marked "low n". A row with n = 0 shows a dash and is marked low n too. Nothing is hidden, merged or extrapolated. |
| **Month** | The UTC calendar month of the cohort day. The table covers the current month and the 12 before it. |

Percentages are rounded to whole numbers, so a row's three percentages may not
add up to exactly 100. The counts always do.

### A fact-checker published a rating

This column is **not measured**. WorldMonitor looks up published fact-checks
only when someone checks a claim on [/verify](https://www.worldmonitor.app/verify)
(see [Revision and Corrections Log](/corrections)), and those lookups are not
linked to stories. Measuring it would need a fact-check lookup for every story
in every cohort, which spends the Google Fact Check Tools API quota. Until that
is decided, the column says "Not measured" with this reason, rather than being
left out.

If it is measured later, it will count stories for which an IFCN-verified
fact-checker published a rating of a strongly matching claim, whatever the
rating says. WorldMonitor will not map ratings onto a true/false scale.

## When a day is counted

A cohort day is counted once every story in it has passed its 7-day checkpoint:
from 8 days after the day starts, plus a one-hour margin for digest writes in
flight. Until then, its stories are not in the table at all, so a month's
figures grow as its days are counted. Each month lists how many cohort days it
counts and which.

The figures are computed once per cohort day and never revised. A later change
to a story, after its checkpoints, does not change the table.

### Days left out

The digest writes at most 60,000 events per day. If a day reaches that limit,
every cohort day whose outcomes depend on it is left out of the table, and the
month says how many days were left out. A day on which the digest recorded
nothing (for example before this table existed) has no record and is not
counted.

## Reproducibility

The table is computed only from logged label history.

1. **Events.** The full English digest appends one line per event to a list for
   the event's UTC day, `trust:calibration-events:v1:<YYYY-MM-DD>`:
   `f|<storyId>|<state>|<epoch ms>` for a story's first label, and
   `s|<storyId>|<state>|<epoch ms>` for each later change of its label, where
   `<state>` is `single-publisher`, `corroborated` or `other`. Lists are kept for
   21 days.
2. **Day records.** The `seed-label-calibration` seeder reads a cohort day's list,
   the day before it (so a story logged twice counts once, in the earlier day),
   and the seven days after it, replays each story's events in time order, and
   writes the counts once to `trust:calibration-day:v1:<YYYY-MM-DD>`. Day records
   are kept for 400 days and never overwritten.
3. **Table.** Every six hours the seeder sums the day records of the last 13
   months into `trust:label-calibration:v1`, which
   `GET /api/news/v1/list-label-changes` serves as `calibration`.

Within the 21-day window, a day record can be recomputed from its event lists
with the published rules (`shared/label-calibration.js`). After that, the day
record is the logged history.

## Uniform application

Every rule above reads only the label, the story's identity hash and the time.
No topic, person, party, outlet or country changes a definition or a threshold,
and every story in a cohort counts the same.

## Limits

- **Coverage only.** The table measures how labels moved, not whether stories
  were true.
- **English digest only.** Other languages and variants are not counted.
- **Left the digest.** A story that drops out of the digest keeps its last
  label, so "same label" includes stories nobody covered again.
- **Returning stories.** A story whose record expired after 7 days without
  coverage and that then returns is counted again, as a new story.
- **Event loss.** If an event write fails, a label change is retried on the next
  digest cycle, but a lost first label is not: that story is missing from its
  cohort, which lowers n without changing any outcome.
