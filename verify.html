How this works

  • WorldMonitor matches your text against the live headlines it has collected from its curated feeds.
  • Feeds that belong to one newsroom (Reuters and Reuters World, say) count as one publisher.
  • Each source carries a declared tier and, where reviewed, a state-media or caution label.
  • “Not found” means not in WorldMonitor’s current sources. It never means false.
  • Below the coverage, WorldMonitor quotes ratings that IFCN-verified fact-checkers have published about a similar claim, with their name, date and link. The rating is theirs; WorldMonitor does not rate claims, and a rating never changes the coverage result.
